# Gate v3: promoted routers, region families, per-family side-effect parameters, per-order gate subject

Status: accepted (2026-10-30, user decisions; add-gate-v3, design D1–D8, task 1.2). Open
Questions 1–3 of the design are still to be answered by the user after the pilot; the defaults
named below stand until then.

## Context

The frozen v2 gate set (EXP-0047: registry v2, roster v2, X 0.7516 / Y 0.7302) has three gaps.

- **The bar is weaker than our own evidence.** Roster v2's six members are all `entity_planner`
  with splitting or blind routers. `round_robin` beats `random` on all 104 random-dominated v2
  episodes (EXP-0048). `makeable_weighted_whole` (MWW) beats `round_robin` on 62% of the 639 atlas
  screen worlds (EXP-0051 addendum). These routers were diagnostic only (M3 D3), so the gate never
  saw them. The user's standing rule is that the roster holds the strongest hand-written policy
  found.
- **The registry sits where routing matters least.** Every v2 product has two makers at light
  load. Confirmed headroom is a 42-producer wide-choice (`sparse_f14`) region, plus a smaller
  12-producer dense / f4 region at load (EXP-0052, task 5.3 addendum).
- **The gate cannot judge the M4 orderer.** M4's candidate is a sampled per-order policy under
  the `choice-v1` decode (ADR-0059). The gate accepts only `portable` or a baseline pair.

Every atlas world is already taken. M4 trains on the train split, selects on validation and
confirms once on held-out and held-out-region (add-m4-orderer-redesign D6 / D9). M4 D6 also found
that v2's absolute X / Y would flag the bar arms themselves on loaded worlds, where the best simple
rule fills about 46% of orders (EXP-0052).

## Decision

### 1. Roster v3: five routers promoted, pruning decides (design D1)

- `round_robin`, `even_split_rotating`, `random_whole`, `makeable_weighted_whole` and
  `makeable_split` join `ORDERING_BASELINES` under their existing names. They are the same
  function objects as in `DIAGNOSTIC_ROUTERS`, which keeps them for the audits. No existing
  baseline changes behaviour: both dictionaries bind the same function objects, and the move was
  verified against base 43f77c6 by action digests in the task 1.1 review (no test pins them).
- **No import cycle.** The routers and their helpers live in `eval/rotation_routers.py`, which
  imports neither `baselines` nor `routing_diagnostics`. `routing_diagnostics` re-exports them.
- **Stochastic members.** A member is stochastic when either policy is in
  `STOCHASTIC_BASELINES = {random, random_whole, makeable_weighted_whole}`
  (`roster.is_stochastic`). It runs R = 5 replicates and is valued at the replicate mean. The
  stored member field keeps the name `random`, so v1 / v2 hashes do not change.
- **Draft.** 8 producing × 11 ordering baselines = 88 members, 32 stochastic.
- **Pruning, not a hand list.** A promoted router stays only if, with some producer, it sets the
  per-episode best on at least one development episode (rule `never-argmax-two-wave-v1`, D3).
- **Hand-written only (user decision).** Trained checkpoints (the EXP-0012 orderer, the portable
  stack, M4's candidate) and the `d2` teacher are never members. The "strongest policy" rule
  applies to hand-written policies only.
- **Strongest-policy rule (user decision, operative).** The roster always holds the strongest
  hand-written policy found. One found later that beats every member enters the v3 draft if it is
  found before the v3 screen, and otherwise the next roster version, through a draft plus a
  baseline-only re-screen. Both remain human-gated roster changes. A pending case: if EXP-0067
  reads `utwf_strongest` (uTWF beats `round_robin` and MWWk on atlas worlds),
  uTWF (`routing_diagnostics.utwf`) is a candidate for such a policy, brought to the user.

### 2. Registry v3: v2 unchanged plus two region families of fresh draws (design D2)

- Every v2 entry stays as it is. Two families are added:
  - `ws_wide`: 42 producers, choice `sparse_f14`;
  - `ws_loaded`: 12 producers, choice alternating `dense` / `sparse_f4`.
- **Fresh draws (user decision).** Region worlds are new draws from the atlas lever distribution
  (`world_space.sample_levers`) on seed streams of their own (`gate-v3-<split>`). No atlas world
  enters a registry. A test proves the seeds are disjoint from v2, the atlas-v1 sample, M4's
  `m4-es-train-v1` pool and the other split.
- **Counts.** Per split, size and regime, region worlds are one third of the group (a default;
  design Open Question 1 reopens the weight after the pilot): development
  4 calm + 2 shocked, sealed 8 + 4, plus reserves. Calm stays twice shocked.
- **Regimes.** Calm region worlds are cyclic and shock-free. Shocked ones carry `shocks-i` and
  its drift exactly, so the shocked regime stays one population.
- **Acceptance filter.** A draw is kept when its measured ρ lies in (0.28, 3.1]. 0.28 is M4's
  training window (add-m4-orderer-redesign D6 / D9); 3.1 is the top of the confirmed-hard range.
  Whether `ws_wide` keeps the lower cut is design Open Question 2, decided after the pilot.
  Rejected draws are recorded. An empty pool refuses the build.
- Each region entry stores its world record, and loading checks the replayed config hash.
- `GATE_FAMILIES` is version-aware: v3 admits `sparse_wide`, `ws_loaded` and `ws_wide`.

### 3. The v3 development screen (design D3)

- **Pilot first.** An `entity_planner` × 11 routers dry-run screen measures headroom,
  attainability and a per-world routing reading before the screen's spend. It never prunes or
  calibrates.
- **Two waves.** Wave 1 runs all 88 members on the v2 and `ws_loaded` worlds. Wave 2 runs the
  wave-1 winners plus `entity_planner` × every router on the costly `ws_wide` worlds. No stack
  without `entity_planner` set the per-episode best in EXP-0044 attempt 2 or EXP-0047.
- **Replacement.** Zero-baseline replacement as today. Region worlds also get bar-death
  replacement: a world whose argmax member dies is replaced by the next reserve.
- **Per-family admission.** Headroom (median argmax fill < 0.95) is checked per family × size. A
  failing family × size is `excluded` in both splits and recorded; the others proceed. Rows of
  excluded worlds are reported only.
- Attainability and the routing reading are reported, never gating.

### 4. X / Y per family (design D4, user decision)

- `side_effect_params_v3` records X and Y per family: `sparse_wide` (both sizes pooled), `ws_loaded`
  and `ws_wide`. Each is the nearest-rank q = 0.05 quantile of the argmax member's run-down ratio
  and per-consumer fill rates over that family's development episodes.
- A world is checked against its family's values. v1 / v2 files keep scalar X / Y, read unchanged.
- A family with fewer than 20 distinct values is flagged `small_sample`. The rule does not change.

### 5. Sealed split screened at 12 producers (design D5, user decision)

- Sealed region worlds are drafted now, from their own seed stream.
- `--mode screen-sealed --sizes 12` runs the frozen v3 roster on the 12-producer sealed groups,
  with replacement only. No pruning or calibration: those use development evidence only.
- `--scope-sizes 12` makes a real report size-scoped. The scope is recorded in `spec.json` and in
  the pre-registration manifest.
- **The M4 exit read is the sealed 12-producer split, both regimes (user decision).** The
  development read on v3 is reported first. 42-producer sealed screening waits for M5.

### 6. The per-order gate subject (design D6, user decision)

- `--subject per-order` takes a `per-order-composite-v1` checkpoint, a white-box deciding
  producer and a reported producer checkpoint.
- **`entity_planner` decides (user decision).** Its rows alone enter the verdict. The gate judges
  the ordering role M4 trains (EXP-0054: c_O = 1.02, c_P = 0.12), and every roster member carries
  the same producer.
- **EXP-0035 is reported.** The same orderer with the EXP-0035 producer gives `candidate:reported`
  rows and `reported.csv`, split off before the verdict and labelled "reported, not gating".
- **R = 5, judged on the mean (user decision).** Replicate r sets
  `replicate_seed(world, episode, r)` and `DrawKey(world, episode, r)`. The world ratio, run-down
  and starvation use replicate means, as the random roster members do.
- **Deaths per replicate.** A death has no mean, so a death in any replicate fails the zero-death
  clause. This is the design's reading of "judged on the mean"; it is put to the user as Open
  Question 3 and stands until answered.
- **Env.** Candidate jobs use M4's `per_order_scorer.per_order_env`, never a gate-local copy.
- **Pins.** `per_order_scorer.per_order_pins` by reference (decode pin included), plus the gate's
  own pins: composite SHA-256 and format, M4 pre-registration hash, deciding producer, reported
  producer SHA-256, R and size scope. A choice-v1 report never resumes onto a logits-v1 out-dir.
- An invalid or non-finite choice is a `job` contract failure; the run is invalid.

### 7. The 42-producer teacher read (design D7)

- `d2` and the PoC-C role swap run on the 42-producer `sparse_f14` atlas train worlds, sized by a
  local probe and a pre-registered reduction ladder (L1–L5).
- It is a reference for the orderer at 42: a lower bound on what lookahead reaches. It is never
  an acceptance read, a label source, a training target or a gate input.
- `ub` is not run (see Alternatives).
- It passes roster v2 explicitly, so its bar and reach rows reproduce EXP-0052 / EXP-0057.

### 8. Records and human gates (design D8)

- The screen writes candidate frozen files into its EXP dir only. Packaging them as
  `eval/registry/*_v3.json` waits for the user's approval.
- **Packaging does not move the defaults.** `DEFAULT_REGISTRY`, `DEFAULT_ROSTER` and the default
  parameter file stay v2 while M4 runs. Every v3 command passes the v3 paths explicitly.
- The sealed-12 screen and both gated reads wait for the user's approval of v3. The gated reads
  also wait for M4's `confirmed` (EXP-0061).

## Consequences

- The bar rises, most on the worlds where the deployed scorer trails (EXP-0051 addendum). The
  screen's record reports the v2-roster bar beside the v3 bar on v2 worlds.
- Region worlds are narrower than the atlas region: calm is cyclic only, shocked is `shocks-i` +
  drift only. Burst, iid and seasonal demand stay atlas and M4 evidence.
- Safety is relative per family. On loaded worlds the starvation floor is far lower than on
  `sparse_wide`, matching what the baselines achieve there.
- `sparse_wide` X / Y are recalibrated with the v3 roster, so their values change.
- Region families are small (32 development world-episodes each). More worlds are a cost decision
  for the user.
- **M4 commit timing.** Gate-v3 commits change the package source hash. M4 refuses to cross it
  in `order_es` train resume (`source_tree_sha256`) and in `order_es_validate
  --reuse-references` (`REUSE_PINS`). So gate-v3 code commits land only between M4 runs: never
  between an M4 seed-0 read and its seed-1 read, and never under a train run that will be resumed.
- **gate_cli carry-forwards** (binding, from the task 1.1 review):
  - `gate_cli` chooses baseline-subject replicates in `build_jobs` and in the
    `candidate_replicates` pin with `RANDOM_POLICY in pair`. A baseline subject with
    `makeable_weighted_whole` or `random_whole` would get one unseeded replicate. Both switch to
    `roster.is_stochastic(*pair)`, with a test, before any read with a baseline subject.
  - The pilot roster (`PILOT_V3_NAME`) carries version tag 3. `gate_cli` must refuse it outside
    `--dry-run`, or a real screen would freeze it as `roster-v3`.
- Moving the default to v3 is a follow-up after M4 closes: every driver that reads a default is
  first pinned to an explicit version, with tests, and the user approves the switch.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Atlas worlds in the registry.** Every hard world of both regions is in one of M4's four
  splits. Train or validation worlds would let the gate read worlds M4 trained or selected on;
  held-out worlds would burn M4's one confirmation.
- **Unselected EXP-0051 screen worlds.** The atlas selection left them as the lower-scoring
  remainder of each bin, so they are a biased sample. The EXP-0051 addendum regressions that
  shaped M4 were fitted on them. Each has one screen episode only.
- **One pooled X / Y over all v3 worlds.** Its value would be set by the arbitrary 2 : 1 family
  weighting. It would either flag region baselines or loosen the `sparse_wide` checks.
- **Trained roster members** (the EXP-0012 orderer, the portable stack, M4's candidate). The
  roster is hand-written stacks by definition, and M4 already compares its candidate to the
  trained orderer at validation and held-out. The user confirmed the rule.
- **Running `ub` at 42.** In EXP-0053 it landed 1–99 units below `d2` on all 5 worlds where it
  ran (DIAGNOSTICS 23), so it is no upper bound for this search, and it costs a full search
  episode. The atlas ceilings (B2, B_lp, C) are the upper references instead.
- **Moving the defaults during M4.** `order_es_worlds`, `order_es`, `split_need`, `teacher_audit`,
  `decoder_check`, `atlas` and `routing_audit` read the packaged defaults. Moving them would break
  or silently change M3 / M4 runs.
