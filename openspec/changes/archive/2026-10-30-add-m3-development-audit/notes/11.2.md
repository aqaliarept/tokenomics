and gain ≥ 2/3 · gap (TW − TR = splitting; RS − TR = receiver rule). ≥ 2 of 3 worlds each:
gap clears on < 2 → `no_gap`; TW recovers → `f` (`f+b` if RS too); else RS → `b` (receiver rule:
concurrent spread and / or rotation over time); else gap clears and neither single change
recovers → `joint` (only whole + `round_robin` receivers together; interaction tells complements
from additive halves); else `neither` (worlds disagree; diagnose world by world). Matched
contrasts RR − RS, RR − TW (with shares of the gap) and the interaction always reported.

RR's 0.92), confirming the forced-whole arm alone confounds splitting with rotation. Concurrent
top share TR 1.0, TW 1.0, RS 0.63, RR 0.55, RW 0.57–0.64: the scorer sends all concurrent orders
of a product to one producer (tiled scores), so the receiver factor is mostly concurrent spread.

  `exp0054/`. Each needs `episodes.csv` or `artifacts/episodes.csv`; `spec.json` is not read.

  pass `--reference` to a smoke run against real EXP dirs.
