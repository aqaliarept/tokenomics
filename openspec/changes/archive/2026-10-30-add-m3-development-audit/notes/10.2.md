## Check on the pod's `spec.json`

- `host.vcpu` must equal the pod's allocation (8). The driver takes the affinity mask capped by
  the cgroup quota; a container may report the host's CPUs in `os_cpu_count`. If `vcpu` reads
  < 8, `--workers 6` is refused at start (workers ≤ vcpu − 2): relaunch is a new decision, not a
  silent `--workers` cut, since the 2-idle-core protocol is about the real cores. If `vcpu` reads
  > 8 (host CPUs leaking through), the timings ran with fewer idle cores than the driver thinks;
  record it in RESULT.
- Record `host.cpu_model` and the run commit in RESULT (11.1 Verify).
- `loadavg_start` / `loadavg_end` in `host`: a load above ~7 on 8 vCPU means contention beyond D7's
  design; say so next to the slopes.
