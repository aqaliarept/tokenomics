  least 1. A container may report the host's CPUs in `os.cpu_count`; `spec.json` `host` records
  `os_cpu_count`, `affinity`, `cgroup_cpu_quota`, `cgroup_cpu_raw` and the `vcpu` used. 1 worker
  on this 2-vCPU box; 6 on a `cpu8` pod if the pod's mask / quota say 8 (no pod record yet:
  10.2 / 11.1 should read `host` on the first pod run). `--workers 1` runs in-process.
- The out dir must be fresh (`spec.json` present → refused; no resume). The run is small enough
  that a rerun is cheaper than resume logic.
- 10.2's probe: `--dry-run --seeds 1 --repeats 1 --horizon 12 --workers 1` (all 8 cells by
  default). `timings.csv` has `wall_s` per episode plus `setup_s` (config + env build, outside

## Gotchas

- **Decisions per cycle grow with fanout, on the orderer side only** (review round 1, concern 1).
  At P = 42: E 2 → 148, E 4 → 203, E 7 → 287, E 14 → 483 decisions per cycle. Producer decisions
  stay at 42 per cycle; orderer streams go 106 → 441. Pooled per-decision time therefore
  dilutes a producer-side stage (1.5 per producer decision shows as ≈ 0.6 pooled), and dividing
  the per-cycle stages (`domain`, `harness`) by the growing count pulls their slopes down by
  about 0.6. Measures (`MEASURES`, `spec.json` `pre_registered.measures`):
  - `decision`: every stage over all decisions (D7's literal quantity). Flagged.
  - `prod_decision` / `order_decision`: `obs`, `forward`, `decode` charged to the role whose
    call it is, over that role's decisions. Flagged.
  - `cycle`: every stage per cycle. Reported, never flagged.
  `slopes.csv` has an `axis, measure, stage` row for each; `cells.csv` has every measure column.
  11.1's RESULT should read the per-role rows for the bounded per-decision rule, and say which
  measure each quoted number is.
- **Role attribution**: `observe` charges the agent's role; `producer_entity_view`,
  `decode_producing`, `producing_agent_action` and the producer model's `forward_view` are
  producer; `product_stream_view`, `decode_ordering`, `ordering_agent_action` and the orderer
  model's `forward_view` are orderer. Producer + orderer seconds equal the pooled policy-side
  stage (test).
- **Stage mapping** (module docstring): `obs` = env `observe` + `System.producer_entity_view` /
  `product_stream_view` (the policies read the views again; both count); `forward` = the
  scorers' `forward_view`, row-feature tensorization included; `decode` = the policy's own work
  after its forward (producer planner, action packing) + env `decode_producing` /
  `decode_ordering` (with `allocate_suborders`) + `producing_agent_action` /
  `ordering_agent_action`; `domain` = `start_ordering` + `complete_cycle`; `harness` = the rest,
  `reset` included. Exclusive accounting: stages sum to the timed wall exactly up to the
  `perf_counter` gaps (test: within 5%).
- **Instrumentation is per-episode and reversible**: instance attributes on the env, its System
  (re-installed after every `reset`) and the models, plus the `env.aec_env` module's
  `decode_producing` / `decode_ordering` globals, all restored on exit. The module patch is
  process-global while an episode runs: never time two episodes in threads of one process.
- **Timer overhead**: one `perf_counter` pair per wrapped call (several per decision; not measured separately). The
  timed wall is what the probe and the pod pay; size from it, not from an untimed episode.
- **Pod staging**: the checkpoints live under repo-root `experiments/`, which is not synced.
  Stage them under the git-ignored `tokenomics/_pod_ckpts/` (not `_pod_inputs/`, which breaks
  `--verify-source-commit`; EXP-0053 RESULT). `--verify-source-commit` resolves locally; pass
  the result to the pod as `--source-commit`.
- **Calm worlds** play a fixed demand schedule; the episode seed (0) only seeds the action
  spaces, so repeats differ only in timing noise. Same world seeds as the family ratio sample.
- **Contention**: with `--workers` > 1 the episodes share memory bandwidth; D7 leaves 2 cores
  idle for that. Repeats are submitted repeat-major so a load spike cannot hit all three
  repeats of one cell.
