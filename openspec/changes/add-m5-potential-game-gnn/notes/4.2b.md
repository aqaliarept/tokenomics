It differs from v2 only in `guard` (plus `version` and `changes`).

**Rule (`score_member_v3`):**
- **Deaths are absolute.** A member episode that dies where rf3 dies at no key (r0..r4) disqualifies the member.
  In EXP-0080 the only such world-episode was 12-0022:1267846658, where rf3 died at r0.
- **Flags.** An episode is flagged when v2's non-death clauses (run-down / starvation beyond the margin, against
  rf3's r0) fire. The expected count on a minibatch is the mean over rf3's re-keys r1..r4 of their flagged
  episodes on the same world-episodes. These come from EXP-0080's `noise_episodes.csv`, read next to
  `margins.json`.
- **Disqualified** if flags(member) − expected > T.

**Calibration (`--mode flag-calibrate`, EXP-0080 rows only):**
- Leave-one-out: re-key k's count against the mean of the other three.
- Minibatches: the 82 minibatches the registered runs draw at size 24 (the two probe minibatches, plus opt seeds
  0 and 1 × generations 1..40 from M4's stream). That gives 328 samples.
- T = the smallest t (0 or an observed excess) with share(excess > t) ≤ 5%: **T = 5/3**.
- **rf3's self-disqualification: 9 / 328 = 2.7%.** That is 41% without the threshold. The max excess is 8/3.
- Because counts are discrete, the next lower t exceeds 5%.
- Sources (pinned; a real probe / train run refuses other files):
  - `noise_episodes.csv` `cc6ef405…`
  - `margins.json` `561c94e2…`
  - `bar.csv` `c42b9ecc…`

**Scope:** the rule applies to probe and train member scores. The monitor and the L5 / L6 reads are unchanged.
