# 4.2e: why EXP-0081's members die (death diagnostic, 2026-11-05)

**Run as:** local one-shot traces of single world-episodes at horizon 100 (plus 200 for the counterfactual), a
few seconds each, on the recorded keys and the probe's θ.
- Scripts: `probes/death_trace.py`, `probes/death_summary.py`, `probes/death_counterfactual.py` (output
  `.txt`).
- The wrappers are eval-side and read-only, except the counterfactual's veto, which exists only in the probe.
- No rule, pre-registration or scoring changed.

## Plain English
The members do not die from anything the probe should punish. **They fall into an upgrade trap that rf3 is
already in.**

**How the trap works:**
- A producer's capacity degrades each cycle by a fixed **percentage of its max capacity**.
- A restoration returns a **fixed** number of units, and at most one runs at a time.
- So each producer has a largest sustainable max: restores / degradation %.
- The planner (`entity_planner`, inside rf3) upgrades whenever demand pressure exceeds max capacity. It never
  checks that ceiling.
- Past the ceiling, even a restoration every cycle loses to degradation, and the producer drains steadily.

**On 12-0016:**
- Product 0 (and every producer's restoration and downstream expansion) depends on tier 0.
- Tier 0 is effectively **one producer, p0_0**:
  - p0_1 and p0_3 have max capacity 1, below the 3–4 units a product needs. They keep the products formally
    "makeable" but make nothing.
  - p0_2 degrades at 10% and sits at 15 / 234.
- p0_0's ceiling is 16 / 6% = 266. rf3 upgrades it to 303 by cycle 80, so it loses about 3 per cycle (155 at
  cycle 60, 59 at cycle 100).
- **rf3 itself dies at cycle 115 when the episode runs to 200.** The 100-cycle horizon hides it.
- The members load tier 0 a little more. The expansion trigger θ_e asks for 728 downstream expansions against
  rf3's 67, each needing product 0. That raises demand pressure on p0_0, so the planner upgrades it further
  (max 311–327) and the drain arrives earlier: death at cycles 87–99.

**On 12-0004 (shocked, sparse, 2 makers per product):**
- Product 3's makers are p0_2 (ceiling 24 / 8% = 300) and p0_3 (max 1).
- rf3 upgrades p0_2 to 288, just under the ceiling, and holds it at 170.
- Member +06 upgrades it to 336 by cycle 40; it drains to 0 by cycle 91.

**Which correction:** single-part ablations (θ_k, θ_b or θ_e alone, the others 0):
- θ_e alone reproduces −00's death (cycle 98), and drives −05 to r_end 0.21.
- θ_b alone is inert on these worlds (its extra rounds to 0).
- θ_k alone survives and often gains units (+06/k +293 on 12-0004).
- +03 and +06 die only with all three combined.

| 12-0016 | rf3 | p0_0 303 | 266 | — / **115** |
| 12-0016 | −00 / +03 / −05 / +04 / −11 | 311–327 | 266 | 87–99 |
| 12-0004 | rf3 | p0_2 288 | 300 | — / — |

**Restorations are not the failure.** On 12-0016, p0_0's restorations succeed about 0.97 per cycle (rf3 70 of
72; members 70 of 71). Tier 0's share of the investment fund is unchanged (0.19 vs 0.21). The restorations
are simply too small for the upgraded max.

**Counterfactual** (veto an upgrade whose post-upgrade degradation ceil(d% · (max + increases)) exceeds the
restoration's units; `probes/death_counterfactual.txt`):

| world | arm | H100 units as-is → veto | H200 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12-0016 | rf3 | 1,886 → **3,500** | dies at 115 → lives, 8,531 |
| 12-0016 | 5 dying members | 1,488–1,748 (dead) → 3,121–3,695 (alive) | all alive |
| 12-0004 | rf3 | 3,282 → **4,514** | 4,289 → 9,977 |
| 12-0004 | +06 | 2,747 (dead) → 4,563 (alive) | alive |

- With the veto, no arm dies on either world at either horizon.
- **rf3's own units roughly double**, because the trap drains rf3 too.

**World coordinates:**
- **12-0016:** ρ 6.57, dense, 4 nominal makers per product (1 effective at tier 0), capacity_scale 0.27,
  cv_capacity 0.75, cv_degradation 0.55, no shocks, cyclic demand. rf3's min capacity ratio is 0.30.
- **12-0004:** ρ 3.75, sparse_f2, shocks (0.25 events per producer, hit 0.36, outage 7–8.5), iid demand.

## What would prevent it
- **A producer rule, in the base and not in the scoring:** do not upgrade past the sustainable max
  (restores / degradation %).
  - It is a flaw of the base planner. rf3 itself dies on 12-0016 at 115 and loses about half its units to the
    trap on both worlds.
  - It would change the base, so L1 (EXP-0080) and the probe would need re-running on the new base.
  - It is a planner / base design decision for the user. How general it is across the 128 training
    world-episodes is untested; that is an experiment (pod).
- **Constraining the corrections would not fix the cause.** The residual has no upgrade knob, and rf3 dies
  without any correction. A smaller θ_e only delays the drain.
- **The worlds are fragile only through the trap.** A single effective tier-0 maker is a real fragility, but
  with the veto both worlds survive 200 cycles.
