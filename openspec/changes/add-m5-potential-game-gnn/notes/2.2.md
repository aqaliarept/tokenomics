# 2.2: ES driver (`train/producer_es.py`)

## Decision record (supervisor, 2026-11-05, before any 2.2 code)
- **Fitness is consumer units only,** relative to the bar (the base rf3 + MWWk on the same
  world-episode and draw key), with no holding term. This is design D3 ("scores whole-episode
  consumer units directly"), and it matches what the gate measures. M4's `order_es` fitness
  subtracted h · H (h 0.1); that term is not used here.
- **Guard:** the run-down / starvation disqualification stays as designed (D5), relative to rf3,
  with its margin measured on rf3's own re-keyed replays (L1).
- **Diagnostics in every generation and every read:** holding units per cycle and the investment
  rejection rate, for the members and the base. Reported, never in the fitness or a reading.
- **Registered sanity check `stock_heavy`** (in the pre-registration, task 2.3):
  - flagged when the selected θ's mean held stock (holding units per cycle) exceeds rf3's by more
    than 50% on the read's world-episodes;
  - reported only, never deciding;
  - the supervisor takes a flag to the user, since the domain treats carried inventory as a
    liability.
