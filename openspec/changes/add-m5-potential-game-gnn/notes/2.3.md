## Supervisor decisions (2026-11-05), folded into 2.3's review
1. **The normaliser (design D2 gap).**
   - M4's median / IQR normaliser, clipped at ±5, with M4's zero-spread rule (IQR → population
     std → 1.0), on every non-bias feature.
   - Fitted on rf3's own raw feature rows: the `bar` run plays the residual at θ = 0 (equal to rf3
     action for action) with a recorder, on every producer-cycle of the 128 training
     world-episodes.
   - Pinned by SHA-256 in `normaliser.json`. Every probe / train / monitor job carries it, with
     its SHA checked.
   - θ = 0 still equals rf3. The bar's rows are the residual's θ = 0 rows, and the noise floor's
     r0 replay of the registered rf3 policy must reproduce them.
2. **The L2 probe at σ = 0.05 and 2σ = 0.1.**
   - Same ε, minibatches and keys; only σ differs.
   - The pilot is licensed by arm S's reading; M4's step reading (`order_es.step_reading`, as in
     EXP-0069) decides its σ.
   - The probe's cost doubles, within M5-P; task 3.1 re-sizes it.
3. **The monitor aligned to M4:**
   - the centre and the base (rf3) at replicates 0 and 1 on the 16 monitor world-episodes, every
     10 generations and at generation 0;
   - the base rows are run once;
   - g_tr = the equal-weight mean of mean_r (centre − rf3);
   - F_tr follows M4's floors rule (computed by the reads, task 2.4).

    section equal to the code's registration.
