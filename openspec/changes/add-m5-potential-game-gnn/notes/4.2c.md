# 4.2c: pre-registration v4, the penalised death (2026-11-05, before EXP-0083)

**Decision:** the user's "Penalised death" (notes/user-decisions.md), made after EXP-0082. v1, v2 and v3 are
untouched. v4 is `es_prereg_m5_v4.json`, SHA `4a99c824…`, pinned as `producer_es.PREREG_SHA256`.

**What changes from v3:**
- `guard.rule` and `guard.death`.
- `fitness.death`.
- Everything else is unchanged: the flag-count rule and T = 5/3, the monitor, and the L5 / L6 reads.

**The rule (`score_member_v4`):**
- A member episode that dies where rf3 dies at no key (r0..r4) scores (units − P) / B_we, with **P = B_we**
  (rf3's r0 units: the supervisor's default). That is units / B_we − 1.
- Such a death never disqualifies; the member ranks by its fitness.
- A death where rf3 also dies at some key (EXP-0080: 12-0022:1267846658) scores its units, unpenalised, as in v3.
- No alternative P was proposed: the default ties the penalty to the episode's own scale, with no free constant.
