## Why the probe did not run
- **What happened.** The between-steps check (reading `spec.json` / `normaliser.json` /
  `bar.csv` from the pulled copy) ran on this box, which was thrashing: load 8.8, from other
  agents' work. A ~2-second Python read took about 14 minutes, from 13:24 to 13:38, while the
  pod sat idle and billing.
- **Where that left the pod.** At 13:38:22 it had 21.3 min of pod life, against the 22.5-min
  hard stop (6.0 vCPU-h on cpu16). The probe (≈ 3–5 min plus restart) could not fit.
- **What I did.** I deleted the pod. The first `runpodctl remove pod` hit a TLS handshake
  timeout; `runpodctl pod delete` succeeded at 13:39:42. `runpodctl pod list` = [].
- **Billed:** 22.65 min × 16 vCPU ≈ **6.04 vCPU-h**, which is 0.04 over the 6.0 stop because
  of the failed first delete. Tranche A billed so far ≈ 5.1 + 6.0 ≈ 11.1 of 100.
- **Root cause.** The between-steps check depended on the shared local box's responsiveness
  while the pod billed. It should not.
  - Run the check on the pod itself, either over SSH or chained into step 1's `--train`.
  - Or give the probe its own pod, staging the pulled bar.
