## Gotchas for 4.2 / 4.3

- **Emitting a `choice-v1` action**: a float32 vector of length `k_stream · P`, reshaped
  (k_stream, P). Row k = the k-th order of `sorted(view.incoming)` (the same order as
  `routing_diagnostics._slots`); a single `1.0` at `spec.producer_ids.index(chosen)`; every other
  entry 0, including rows of empty slots. Eligibility: `env.infos[agent]["action_mask"]` (flat
  int8) or `env.decode.eligibility_mask(env.spec_, view)`.
- Values outside [0, 1] (or NaN) are caught **first** by `ContractMonitor` as `action_space`
  (`ContractViolation`), not as `choice_validity`. Only in-bounds invalid choices reach
  `validate_choice`.
- Orders beyond `k_stream` (v4: 6) are not the policy's: the legacy even split routes them. They
  can be split sub-orders even under a whole-only policy, so "No split emitted" (4.3) must count
  slotted orders only, or assert there was no overflow (`routing_trace` `overflow_orders`).
- "Zero residual equals the decoder check" (4.3): TS under `logits-v1` routes whole through
  `routing_diagnostics._whole` (`split_logits`: log q vs −10). Each other eligible producer's raw
  units are ≈ e⁻¹⁰ ≈ 4.5e-5 whatever q, so the chosen producer floors to q − 1 and takes the
  remainder: exact whole while (n − 1) · 4.5e-5 < 0.5, i.e. any world here. So A0 under
  `choice-v1` (one-hot) and TS under `logits-v1` should give identical sub-orders, provided A0
  draws with the same keyed uniform and inverse CDF as `ordering_sampled_whole`.
- The ordering baselines (`ORDERING_BASELINES`, roster routers) emit logits; they are invalid
  under `choice-v1`. A `choice-v1` env must play choice policies only.
- `ChoiceShareSpace.sample()` without a mask draws over all producers and is usually invalid; use
  `sample(info["action_mask"])` (that is what the `random` baseline would need under `choice-v1`).
- Deep copies (`future_fork`, rollouts) carry `ordering_decode` with the spec; `shock_posterior`
  calls `decode_ordering(env.spec_, ...)`, so it follows the env's decode automatically.
- ADR-0054 ("Corrected product-stream orderer backlog bound (k_stream)") exists in history but
  not in this branch's tree: cleanup commit 18c717d deleted it (read it with
  `git show 18c717d^:docs/adr/0054-corrected-product-stream-backlog-bound.md`). Design D4 cites it
  correctly; ADR-0059 item 4 now cites it too. (Round 1 of this note wrongly said it did not exist
  and attributed k_stream to ADR-0052.)
