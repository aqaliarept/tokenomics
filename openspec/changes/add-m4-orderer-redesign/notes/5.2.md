### log1p on size-scaled counts (supervisor decision 3)

**Which columns, and why.** Only ψ `eligible_producer_count` (E) is transformed. φ has no
transformed column.

The criterion: a count whose 42-producer range cannot occur at 12 producers, so a 12-producer
fit can never see it.
- **E** qualifies. On the 23 loaded atlas train worlds at 12, E ∈ {2, 4} (96 / 180 products). On
  the loaded 42-producer worlds it is {2, 4, 7, 14} (126 / 210 / 84 / 456). At 12, E is bounded by
  the layer width.
- **The other config counts** do not scale, so they stay raw. `product_depth` is {0, 1, 2} at
  both sizes, `recipe_input_count` and `recipe_input_units` are {0, 1}, and
  `recipe_capacity_type_count` is {1}.
- **The demand-driven unit volumes** stay raw: requested / reserved / total load, open demand,
  routed / rejected quantities, the history EMAs, own-turn `assigned_units`, and the order
  `quantity`.
  - They grow at 42, because more consumers stand behind each product. An A0 audit (2 worlds
    per size, h12) gave these p90 values:

    | column | 12 | 42 |
    |---|---|---|
    | `requested_load` | 117 | 306 |
    | `recent_routed_qty` | 7.2 | 12.8 |
    | `open_demand_quantity` | 12 | 43 |
    | `routed_ema` max | 14 | 72 |

  - Their range at 12 is set by the demand levers that the 64 training worlds already sample.
  - Their 42 medians sit inside the 12 range, so only tails clip, and ±5 bounds those.
  - `assigned_orders` is 2 → 3, not structural.
  - Transforming them would also change the 12-producer features, which is beyond decision 3.

**Where the transform runs.**
- `transform_phi` / `transform_psi` run inside `PerOrderPolicy.decide` **before** both the sink
  and the normaliser. The fit rows (5.1's `fit_rows/*.npz`) therefore hold log1p(E), and the fit
  happens in the transformed space.
- 5.1's `static_psi_check` transforms the raw config counts the same way.
  `normaliser_static_42.csv` gains the columns `transform` and `at_clip` (|z| ≥ clip, i.e.
  pinned).
- The `static_psi_clip_42` flag now reads `at_clip`. z = 5.0 exactly is pinned, and the old `>`
  test missed it.

**Versions.**
- `ARCHITECTURE_VERSION` is now `orderer-per-order-residual-v2`.
- `NORMALISER_VERSION` is now `median-iqr-log1p-v2`.
- The normaliser dict carries `transforms` (hashed), and `from_dict` refuses another version or
  other transforms. v1 composites and v1 normaliser files are therefore refused (tested).

**What does not change.**
- Parameter counts stay 44 / 268 / 156 (tested).
- A0 == TS is unchanged: the base scores b come from the frozen scorer's own inputs, and A0 never
  reads φ / ψ. The 4.3 byte-identity tests pass unedited.

**Zero-spread fix that came with log1p.**
- np.std of an all-equal column whose value is not a binary fraction is ~1e-16, not 0. For
  example, log1p(2) over 100 rows gives 4.4e-16.
- The fit would then take 1e-16 as the scale and send every other value to the clip.
- Fix: `sd = 0` when `np.ptp(col) == 0`, applied in both `FeatureNormaliser._column_stats` and
  5.1's `column_report`. The rule text in `NORMALISER_PREREG` now says "(all-equal values have
  std 0 exactly)".
- Raw counts never hit this, since 2.0 and 4.0 are exact. log1p made it reachable.

**Test** (`test_log1p_keeps_42_producer_static_counts_inside_the_clip`):
- It fits a normaliser on the 12-producer atlas train worlds' per-product static ψ values
  (transformed).
- It applies 5.1's `static_psi_check` to `reads_42_configs(atlas)`, which returns **23** loaded
  42-producer worlds (all splits). The brief's "13" is probably the train / validation subset;
  the test asserts ≥ 13 and covers all 23.
- Every static column has `at_clip` 0 and |z| < 5.
- E = 14 → z = 2.15 transformed. Untransformed it would be (14 − 4) / 2 = 5.0, pinned; the test
  asserts that too, so it discriminates.

**Residual risk for the real fit (for 5.3 / 6.1).** The real fit is over ψ *decision* rows, not
products. Let p be the share of decisions on E = 2 products.
- For p ∈ [0.25, 0.75], IQR = log1p(4) − log1p(2) = 0.511, and z(E = 14) ≤ 3.15.
- Outside that range IQR = 0, and the std rule gives 0.511·√(p(1 − p)), which pins E = 14 at the
  clip (z ≥ 5).
- The per-product share at 12 is 0.35. The bar run's `normaliser_static_42.csv` /
  `static_psi_clip_42` flag shows the outcome before the probe runs.

## ES loop (`--mode train`)

- **Members.**
  - The center plus n antithetic pairs θ ± σ⊙ε_i.
  - σ = `ArmSpec.sigma()`, the per-parameter vector.
  - ε comes from `NOISE_STREAM = "m4-es-noise-v1"`, keyed by (opt seed, generation): a pure
    function, so a resume redraws the same members.
- **CRN.**
  - Every member of generation g plays schedule.json's `minibatches[opt_seed][g-1]`. It is
    re-derived from the recorded stream on load, and a mismatch is refused.
  - The draw key is DrawKey(world, episode, 0), the bar A0's key.
  - The `weak` minibatch 24, or generations beyond the schedule, come from the stream function.
- **Fitness.** f = mean over the minibatch of U / B_we − 0.1 · H / D_we, with B_we and D_we from
  bar.csv.
- **Relative disqualification** (`disqualifying`), against the bar's A0 row on the same
  world-episode and key:
  - death that A0 survives;
  - r_end < X·r_start when A0 is not run down;
  - a consumer below Y that is not in `a0_starved_consumers` (per consumer).
- **Ranking.**
  - Centered ranks over the 2n members, with average ranks on ties. Ties are common early: a
    small perturbation often changes no draw.
  - Disqualified and non-finite members share the lowest group.
  - The center is played (D6) and logged, never ranked.
- **Update.**
  - The estimate is g_j = Σ_i (r(+i) − r(−i)) ε_ij / (2nσ_j).
  - Adam (lr 0.02, β 0.9 / 0.999, eps 1e-8) ascends g − 0.005·θ. This is weight decay coupled
    into the gradient, as OpenAI-ES's l2coeff (registered as `l2_coupled`).
- **NonFiniteChoice.**
  - `run_es_job` catches it as a row with `nonfinite=true`. The member's fitness is None, it takes
    the worst rank, and a log line is printed.
  - It is never a contract failure: `contract_failures.csv` stays header-only (tested with the
    real worker at θ_τ = −800).
- **Tracer on, no forks.** Each job has a `RoutingTracer` and a `ContractMonitor`. The `trace_*`
  scalars are in the rows, including the overflow counter.
- **Monitor.**
  - At generation 0 and every 10 generations (dry-run `--monitor-every`), the center plays the 16
    monitor world-episodes at replicates 0 **and 1**.
  - A0 plays them once per run dir at both replicates. Its replicate-0 units must equal the
    bar's `a0_units`; otherwise it is an `a0_reproduction` run failure and the run is invalid.
  - `monitor_summary.csv` holds center / A0 means, `g_tr_raw` and `improvement_10`.
  - In the dry run the center at θ = 0 equals A0 row for row (tested).
- **Digests.**
  - Every row's demand / shock digest must equal the bar's for its world-episode.
  - A mismatch is a `digest_pairing` run row, and the run is invalid with exit 2.
- **Checkpoints.**
  - `checkpoints/gen-NNNN.json` is written after every generation (and after each monitor eval),
    atomically.
  - It holds θ, the Adam state, the training pins, the generation and monitor history, the
    composite, and a content hash.
  - Rows are appended and fsynced as each job lands. A resume replays only the missing jobs of
    the interrupted generation (tested: the rest of generation 3 only).
  - `member_fitness.csv` is rewritten idempotently.
  - `candidate.json` is the final composite, loadable by `load_composite`.
- **Resume.**
  - Same out dir: the pins must be identical, including `--generations`.
  - `--resume-from <gen-NNNN.json | run dir>` into a new dir: the checkpoint's `training_pins`
    must equal this run's. They cover arm, opt seed, pairs, minibatch, monitor cadence, horizon,
    hyper-parameters, σ hash, every input hash (bar.csv, training worlds, schedule, normaliser and
    its fit rows, orderer, prereg, probe, init θ), decode pins, per-order identity, producer and
    the package source tree.
  - The commit text is excluded, so a records-only commit between the pilot and the main run
    resumes; a code change does not.
  - Tested: 2 + 1 = 3 generations, with byte-equal θ, Adam state, history and monitor.
- **Earlier failures stay.** A train or probe dir whose `contract_failures.csv` holds rows from
  an earlier session ends invalid (exit 2) on rerun, following the gate convention (tested).
- **Spec pins** merge `decode_pins("choice-v1")` (`ordering_decode`) and the normaliser
  hashes (`inputs.normaliser_sha256`, `normaliser_fit_rows_sha256`, `per_order.normaliser_sha256`).

## Probe (`--mode probe`)

- **Setup.**
  - Arms A1, A2, each at its registered initial θ: A1 is 0; A2 is u ~ N(0, 0.1), v = 0. Δ = 0 for
    both.
  - Design D7 says "θ = 0". At an A2 θ of exactly 0 the bilinear block has no first-order
    signal, so the training start point is used. 5.3 should confirm this in the prereg text
    (`probe.theta`).
  - n pairs, with ε from `('probe', arm)` on the noise stream.
  - Each member plays both disjoint probe minibatches from schedule.json.
- **Reads per arm.**
  - The cosine of the two minibatches' rank-shaped estimates. An all-zero estimate is
    undefined and reads `no_signal`.
  - The variance ratio of paired (same minibatch) to unpaired antithetic differences.
- **Reading.** `signal` / `weak` (→ minibatch 24) / `no_signal`. A2 < 0.1 while A1 ≥ 0.1 →
  `a2_arm: A2-R2`.
- **Outputs.**
  - `probe.json`.
  - `probe_fitness.csv`: per arm, minibatch and member, the fitness, flags and rank.
    `probe_from_rows` recomputes the whole reading from it (tested).
  - `probe_episodes.csv`.
- **Out dir.** The out dir may contain the bar run (`runs/exp0058/bar`). Only this driver's own
  files are checked for orphans, and `--out-dir` = `--bar-dir` is refused (tested).
