# 7.12: order TTL as a global world setting (code, 2026-11-05)

## What changed
- **Config** (`domain/config.py`): `Configuration.order_ttl: int | None` (JSON `orderTtl`),
  `DEFAULT_ORDER_TTL = 2`, `MAX_ORDER_TTL = 4`. An explicit 2 is normalised to `None`, so a
  default world is the same object, fingerprint and digest as before. `Configuration.ttl` is the
  effective TTL; `validate()` refuses values outside 1..4.
- **Domain** (`order.py`, `system.py`, `orderer_view.py`): every `Order` carries the world TTL
  (`System.order_ttl = config.ttl`, passed at the consumer, replenishment and investment
  creation sites), expiry is `cycle_counter == self.ttl`, and `ttl_remaining` reads the order's
  TTL. There is no per-order TTL: all orders of a world share it. `ORDER_TTL = 2` stays as the
  default constant. `domain/` is still pure.
- **Env** (`spaces.py`, `encode.py`): `EnvSpec.order_ttl` / `order_lifetime`. The seven
  `/ ORDER_TTL` divisions (latencies, order age, TTL remaining) now divide by the world's TTL.
  `order_ttl` is in `_OMIT_WHEN_UNSET` (fingerprint) and `DIGEST_OMIT_WHEN_UNSET` (digest).
- **Sweep driver**: `routing_audit.job_config` applies a job's `order_ttl` (cached per TTL).
  `decoder_check --order-ttl T` (check mode only; refused with `--mode arm-compare`) is the
  TTL-sweep profile:
  - EXP-0057's sample and draw keys, with every world at TTL T;
  - arms within `TTL_SWEEP_ARMS` = (MWWk, RR);
  - no EXP-0052 / 0056 reference comparison, because those ran at TTL 2;
  - D3's readings withheld (`reading.json` `readings_withheld: true`), because EXP-0073 reads
    across the TTL runs;
  - `order_ttl` and `profile: ttl_sweep` are pinned in `spec.json`, and every job carries
    `order_ttl`.

## Observation shapes: the approach taken
The slot count depends on the TTL: a product stream holds up to TTL + 1 orders, so
`k = max(TTL + 1, INVESTMENT_STREAMS + (TTL + 1) · max_rel)` and `k_stream = (TTL + 1) +
INVESTMENT_STREAMS`.

**Taken: the shape follows the world TTL. It is not padded to 4.**
- At TTL 2 every shape is the old one (k 9 / k_stream 6 on phase1), the fingerprint is
  unchanged, and every old checkpoint loads.
- TTL 3 gives k 11 / k_stream 7; TTL 4 gives 13 / 8.

**Rejected alternatives:**
- **Padding to TTL 4 everywhere** would change every default shape and fingerprint. That
  breaks the goldens and every slot-shaped checkpoint, against "default 2 byte-identical".
- **Keeping k = 3** at higher TTLs would push the fourth and fifth live orders of a stream into
  the decode fallback. That would confound the sweep with an uncontrolled routing change.

**Consequence:** at TTL 3 / 4 only shape-free policies run. These are the hand-written stacks
EXP-0073 uses: `entity_planner` × MWWk / RR. A slot-shaped checkpoint on a TTL-3 / 4 world
carries a different fingerprint and is refused, as on any other economy change.

## Left at TTL 2 on purpose (limitations)
- **`train/per_order_scorer.py` `order_features`** still divides by `ORDER_TTL`. The function
  sits inside the normaliser fitting-code hash (`NORMALISER_CODE_DEFS`), and editing it breaks
  prereg v1–v4 (learned in 7.6). Learned per-order policies therefore assume TTL 2. No EXP-0073
  arm uses them.
- **`eval/ceiling_bound.py`** window default `ORDER_TTL`: the LP bound assumes TTL 2. It is not
  part of the sweep.

## 7.13 sizing (EXP-0073)
- **Sample:** EXP-0057's full train sample: 23 worlds at 12 + 14 at 42, 71 world-episodes, confirm
  seeds, horizon 100. A subset is not needed.
- **Arms:** `entity_planner` × {MWWk R = 2 at EXP-0057's keys, RR}, giving 213 jobs per TTL and
  639 for TTL 2 / 3 / 4.
- **Price:** the same job shape as EXP-0067, also 213 jobs on the EXP-0057 world-episodes. Its
  213 jobs ran in ≈ 2.25 min on 16 vCPU, ≈ 0.6 vCPU-h of compute; billed ≈ 2.0 including setup,
  install, pull and stop.
  - Three TTLs on **one pod**: compute ≈ 1.8 at the TTL-2 price, ≤ 2.4 if TTL 3 / 4 episodes run
    up to 1.3× longer (more live orders per stream). Pod overhead stays ≈ 1.4.
  - **Estimate ≈ 4 vCPU-h on cpu16, ≈ 5 on cpu32** (larger tail and overhead per vCPU-h).
  - **Hard stop 10.** Launch-fit: tranche A 88.8 + 10 = 98.8 ≤ 125. Far under 20.
- **TTL 2 reproduces EXP-0057:** the default is byte-identical, so the TTL-2 MWWk / RR rows must
  equal EXP-0057's. 7.13 checks this offline as a neutrality check.
- **Reading:** offline after the pull. Per stack, consumer units at TTL 3 / 4 against TTL 2
  versus the atlas noise floor (12 decides, 42 reported); also expired units, latency and
  run-down.
