### Worlds (D6 "Training worlds")

- **Atlas part:** the 23 loaded (ρ > 0.28) non-collapse 12-producer atlas v2 train worlds. This is
  `decoder_check.sample_views` at 12 producers, sorted by id; each recorded config hash must
  rebuild.
- **Fresh draws:** `world_space.sample_worlds("m4-es-train-v1", 12, 256)`, in index order (the
  fixed redraw order).
  - They use the atlas lever distribution: every D10 lever range, shocks off / on, all 7 demand
    patterns. D6's "with shocks and patterns on" = the shock and pattern axes sampled, not
    shocks forced on (confirmed by the supervisor).
  - ~2/3 pass ρ (172 of 256), so 41 fit with room.
  - The LHS depends on the pool size, so the pool (256) is pinned. A dry run's `--pool` gives
    different draws.
- **Episodes:** 2 per world, `episode_seeds(world, 2, stage="m4-es-train")`. These are fresh
  seeds for the atlas worlds too.
- **Disjoint seed stream** (supervisor addition 4). `reserved_seeds()` covers:
  - every atlas-v1 world seed at 12 (512) and 42 (128). Atlas v2 has the same worlds, so this
    covers every split, validation and held-out included;
  - the EXP-0051 screen episode seeds and the EXP-0052 confirm episode seeds (2 per world, a
    superset);
  - EXP-0050's fanout grid (world + episode seeds);
  - gate registries v1 / v2.

  A draw whose world seed or any training episode seed is reserved is skipped in the fixed
  order (`skipped_reserved_seed`), never perturbed. An atlas world whose training episode seed is
  reserved is refused. Tested: none of the 256 draws collides; every training seed is distinct;
  the schedule's stream seeds are not reserved; an injected collision is skipped.
- **Filters on a draw**, in this order:
  1. ρ ≤ 0.28: offline, from the same formula `world_space.measure` uses (tested equal);
  2. a bar arm dies on a training episode → `dropped_bar_dead`;
  3. world bar fill ≥ 0.95 → `dropped_saturated`. This is the atlas's definition: the fill rate of
     the arm with the best mean units over the world's episodes (MWW as its replicate mean).

  The bar filters are draw filters. Atlas worlds are only replaced when A0 dies.
- **"A0 infeasible"** (D6). A candidate training world (an atlas world, or a draw that passed the
  filters) on which A0 dies on any training episode is `replaced_a0_dead`; the walk then takes the
  next draw. A dropped draw on which A0 also dies counts as a drop, not a replacement.
  - `no_feasible_start` (exit 3, `spec.status`, to the user) when either:
    - replacements exceed ⌊64 / 4⌋ = 16. I read "more than 1/4 of the candidate training worlds"
      as more than 1/4 of the 64-world target;
    - the order is exhausted with fewer than 50 training worlds.
  - Between 50 and 63 at exhaustion, the run completes with fewer worlds (reason recorded).
- **Waves.** `select()` walks the fixed order: every atlas world, then draws while
  accepted + pending < 64. The next wave = every candidate whose outcome is unknown. This gives
  exactly the set "play the whole order, take the first feasible worlds" (tested against that),
  without playing past the last needed draw.
  - Wave 1 = 23 atlas + the first 41 ρ-passing draws.
  - A resume recomputes the waves from `episodes.csv`.
- Every world the walk reached gets its world record (levers, seed, measured coordinates,
  declarers, config hash) in `world_records.json`, whatever its status. Training worlds are
  pinned in `training_worlds.json` (records, episodes, `cost_s`, content hash).

### Normaliser (supervisor addition 1)

- **Fit rows.** A0's raw rows (`policy.sink = RawRows()`), agent-history mode:
  - written per world-episode to `fit_rows/<world>__<episode>.npz`;
  - each file's array SHA-256 is in its `episodes.csv` row (`fit_rows_sha256`), re-checked at
    load.
- **The fit** uses the **training world-episodes only**: after replacement, never dropped or
  replaced worlds (tested: 8 world-episodes fitted, the replaced world's rows excluded).
- **Rule:** 4.3's `FeatureNormaliser.fit`, unchanged.
- **Outputs:**
  - `normaliser.json`: dict, sha256, the pre-registration entry, the rule per column, flags;
  - `normaliser_report.csv`: per column median, q25, q75, IQR, std, scale, rule
    (`iqr` / `std` / `unit`), clip_low / clip_high / clip_fraction at ±5, flags;
  - `normaliser_static_42.csv`: see below.
- **Flags:**
  - `heavy_clip`: clip fraction ≥ 0.25;
  - `constant_raw_units`: the unit rule, i.e. the column is left in raw units;
  - `history_completion_rate_clip`: φ[37], the history `completion_rate`, as opposed to the
    shared φ[14];
  - `static_psi_clip_42`.
- **Where the rule is recorded.** The pre-registration file `es_prereg_m4_v1.json` belongs to
  **task 5.3**, which freezes it. So 5.1 records the clip, the zero-spread rule and the fit-row
  definition in:
  - the bar's `spec.json` (`pins.normaliser` and `pre_registered.normaliser`);
  - `normaliser.json` (`prereg`);
  - the module constant `NORMALISER_PREREG`, which 5.3 copies together with the fitted hash.
- **`--mode fit-normaliser --bar-dir <bar> --out-dir <derived>`** refits offline from a complete
  bar run. It re-hashes the rows, writes `fit.json` with MATCH / MISMATCH (exit 0 / 2) and never
  writes the bar dir (`--out-dir` = `--bar-dir` is refused).
- **Dry-run fit** (2 worlds, h12; not the registered fit):
  - no `heavy_clip`; history `completion_rate` clip 0.0. The 4.3 fixture had 40% at h12 on one
    world; recheck on the real h100 fit;
  - `constant_raw_units` on φ[16] `eligible`, ψ[7] `recipe_capacity_type_count` and ψ[8]
    `eligible_producer_count`;
  - φ[16] is 1 on every fit row (only eligible rows are recorded), so it normalises to 0
    everywhere: a dead feature, harmless.
  - **`eligible_producer_count` clips on 100% of the products of all 13 loaded 42-producer atlas
    worlds** (E up to 14 against a 12-producer median of 2 and scale 1). The real fit will see
    E ∈ {2, 4} at 12 producers (sparse_f2 / f4 / dense with layer width 4), but E = 7 / 14 at 42
    stays far above. Not handled in 5.1. **Decided:** log1p before normalisation, implemented in
    5.2 (see Gotchas). `recipe_capacity_type_count` did not clip at 42 in the dry run.

### Schedule (D6 "monitor", "CRN"; recorded seed stream)

- **Stream:** `SCHEDULE_STREAM = "m4-es-schedule-v1"`. The seed is
  int(sha256("|".join([stream, purpose, …]))[:16], 16), fed to numpy `default_rng`. Every pick
  takes k **distinct worlds** without replacement, then one of each world's 2 training episodes.
- **Monitor:** 16 world-episodes on 16 distinct worlds, replicates (0, 1) (D6: R = 2).
- **Minibatches:**
  - `minibatch(we, opt_seed, generation, size=12)` is a pure function, keyed by opt seed and
    generation, so seed 1 does not reuse seed 0's batches;
  - `schedule.json` lists opt seeds 0 / 1 × generations 1–40;
  - size 24 (the probe's `weak` branch) is a call away.
- **Probe:** two disjoint minibatches of 6 on 12 distinct worlds.
