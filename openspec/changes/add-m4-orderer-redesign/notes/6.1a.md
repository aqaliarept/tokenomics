## Check 3's data side: pair differences and sign agreement

Fitness is units / B_we minus the holding term, a share of the training bar.
d_i = f(+i) − f(−i), the minibatch mean over its 6 world-episodes.

| arm | mean / max \|d_i\|, minibatch 1 | mean / max \|d_i\|, minibatch 2 | pairs with the same sign in both minibatches (of 12) | Pearson r(d¹, d²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 0.027 / 0.062 | 0.037 / 0.136 | **5** | **−0.13** |
| A2 | 0.023 / 0.057 | 0.023 / 0.058 | **6** | **−0.14** |

- A pair's effect on fitness has no consistent direction across the two disjoint sets of worlds:
  sign agreement is 5–6 of 12, and the correlation is slightly negative. That is what the cosine
  showed, now in fitness space.
- **The perturbations do move trajectories.** Members play the same draw key as A0's bar row
  (CRN, `TRAIN_REPLICATE` = 0), yet on every world-episode only 0–2 of 24 members reproduce A0's
  units exactly.
- Per world-episode, the mean |f(+i) − f(−i)| is 0.014–0.12 of the bar (largest on 12-0155, the
  death world, and 12-0004). The 24 members' fitness spread is 0.05–0.96 of the bar.
- **What check 3 needs and the records lack.** Check 3 compares these differences with A0's
  replicate spread on the same world-episode, and **no A0 replicate spread exists**:
  - A0 was played only at replicate 0 on all 12 probe world-episodes (`bar_episodes.csv`: A0
    replicates {0} everywhere);
  - the probe plays members only, at replicate 0;
  - the train monitor's A0 R = 2 rows exist only for the 16 monitor world-episodes, in train
    runs, and none has run.

  So check 3 stops here. It needs A0 at a second (or further) replicate key on the probe's 12
  world-episodes, which is exactly task 6.1b's run (DIAGNOSTICS 25 check 2), so one run serves
  both.

## Per-world breakdown of the disqualifications

The flags fall on **6 of 12 world-episodes**. Each world-episode trips one or two kinds of flag,
not all three. In the gap columns, A0's margin is its value minus the threshold (positive: A0 is
not flagged); the member column is how far below the threshold the flagged members fell
(min / median / max).

| world-episode (minibatch) | flag | A1 members flagged (of 24) | A2 (of 24) | A0's margin | flagged members' value − threshold, min / median / max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12-0005 (1) | run-down (r_end / r_start < X) | 15 | 9 | **+0.0008** | −0.071 / −0.020 / −0.0009 |
| 12-0139 (1) | run-down | 11 | 16 | **+0.0032** | −0.040 / −0.018 / −0.0001 |
| 12-0014 (1) | starvation of c0 / c5 / c8 (fill < Y) | 16 (c0 11, c5 8, c8 24 member-flags over both arms) | 17 | c0 +0.038, c5 +0.025, c8 +0.034 | c0 −0.102 / −0.015 / −0.003; c5 −0.033 / −0.021 / −0.009; c8 −0.077 / −0.037 / −0.0015 |
| 12-0000 (2) | run-down | 14 | 13 | +0.0323 | −0.062 / −0.019 / −0.0005 |
| 12-0044 (2) | run-down; starvation of c9 | 3 run-down, 7 starvation | 5, 9 | run-down **+0.0036**; c9 +0.0355 | run-down −0.064 / −0.011 / −0.003; c9 −0.082 / −0.019 / −0.004 |
| 12-0155 (2) | death | 2 | 0 | (A0 alive) | – |

- **The rule fires where A0 sits on a knife-edge.**
  - On three of the four run-down worlds, A0's ratio clears X by only 0.0008–0.0036; on the
    fourth (12-0000) by 0.032.
  - On the starvation worlds A0's flagged consumers clear Y by 0.025–0.038. 12-0014 has four
    consumers within 0.05 of Y, and 12-0044 one.
  - Members fall below by a median of 0.011–0.037, sometimes by as little as 0.0001.
- On 5 of the other 6 world-episodes A0 itself is already run down, so the run-down flag cannot
  fire there; its unstarved consumers, where there are any, clear Y by ≥ 0.18.
- **The exception is 12-0002.** A0 is not run down there (it clears X by +0.024), yet no member
  was flagged run-down. Meanwhile 12-0000, at a 0.032 margin, drew 27 run-down flags. So
  closeness to the threshold alone does not predict where the flags fall.
- **Hypothesis, not a finding:** the knife-edge margins are why the relative rule ties so many
  members. Check 2 (6.1b) tests it: whether a re-keyed A0 alone falls across the same edges
  (draw noise).

## Which branch the evidence supports

| branch (DIAGNOSTICS 25) | status after 6.1a |
|---|---|
| disqualification-driven | **excluded as the cause of `no_signal`**: check 1 recovers no signal (A1 0.032, A2 −0.132) |
| flat at σ (pair differences within draw noise) | **open**: needs A0's replicate spread (check 3), not in the records |
| heterogeneous (pair differences above noise but disagreeing across minibatches) | **open**: the same missing input |

- Sign agreement at chance and a near-zero correlation fit both open branches.
- Separately from the `no_signal` cause, the flags tie most members, and the same ties would
  arise in a training generation. Whether the knife-edge margins (A0 within 0.001–0.04 of X / Y
  on the flagged worlds; 12-0002 is the counter-case) cause this is a hypothesis. Check 2
  measures the rate on noise alone.

**DIAGNOSTICS 25 status.** A check has discriminated: check 1 excluded one branch. So the entry
is promoted from provisional to "partially diagnosed: disqualification-driven excluded; flat at σ
vs heterogeneous open (6.1b)".

## 6.1b sizing (not launched)

- **What it plays:** A0 (the monitor job, `make_job(kind=MONITOR, arm="A0", theta=0)`) at
  replicates r = 1 … K on the probe's 12 world-episodes (`probe_batches(inp)`), on EXP-0058's bar.
- **What it reads:**
  - check 2: the relative rule applied to A0(r) against A0(r0) = the bar row (`disqualifying(row,
    bar)`), giving the self-disqualification rate per world-episode;
  - check 3: the per-world-episode replicate spread of `we_fitness`, which needs A0(r0)'s fitness
    from the bar row's `a0_units` / `a0_holding_units_per_cycle`, against each pair's |f(+i) −
    f(−i)| per world-episode from `probe_episodes.csv`.
- **K.** The registered check 2 names a single second key (K = 1). For a usable per-world spread
  I recommend K = 4 (r1–r4, 5 replicates with r0). The cost difference is negligible.
- **Jobs:** 12 × K, so 48 at K = 4 (12 at K = 1).
  - At ≈ 3.7 s local per 12-producer A0 horizon-100 episode (§EXP-0058–0060 world model: mean RR
    1.81 s × A0 / RR 2.04), that is 178 s local, 115 s pod.
  - On cpu8: about 6 rounds × 2.4 s ≈ 15 s of compute, plus 60 s parent and pool open.

| shape | estimate (10-min setup, world model) | at 1-min setup | stop (2 × estimate) | launch-fit (12.0 billed so far) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **cpu8 (task tag)** | ≈ 1.5 vCPU-h | ≈ 0.3 | **3.1** | 12.0 + 3.1 = **15.1 ≤ 100** |

- **Driver change: yes, needed, as its own code task before 6.1b** (tasks.md 6.1b). No existing
  CLI plays A0 at chosen replicate keys on chosen bar world-episodes:
  - the bar's `run_bar` plays only replicate 0 and the whole candidate pipeline;
  - `order_es --mode probe` refuses non-residual arms (A0);
  - the train monitor plays A0 on the monitor set inside a train run;
  - `order_es_validate` refuses non-validation worlds.

  A minimal `order_es --mode noise-floor --bar-dir … --replicates K` would:
  - reuse `make_job` / `play` / `run_es_job`, `disqualifying`, `we_fitness`;
  - take its world-episodes from `probe_batches(inp)`;
  - pin the bar, the prereg and the source commit like the probe;
  - write `noise_floor.csv` (rows), `self_dq.csv` and `spread.csv`.

  It needs tests: a dry run, the refusals, and A0(r0) recomputed from the bar row.
- **No local step while the pod bills:** the run's readings are computed on the pod into its own
