## Supervisor addition 1: latency default → `has_completed` indicator (counts change)

- **Choice:** φ gains `has_completed` (1 once the agent's sub-orders to the producer have
  completed any units this episode, from `AgentHistory.totals`). It sits after the 9 history
  features, at φ column 40.
  - φ = 31 + 9 + 1 + 2 = **43**;
  - A1 = 43 + 1 = **44**;
  - A2 (R = 4) = 44 + 4·(43 + 13) = **268**;
  - A2 (R = 2) = **156**.
- **Why not the imputation:** I first implemented it (latency of a pair without completions →
  the fitted median over completed pairs, so it normalises to 0). The fixture fit then
  showed:
  - on 12-0060 (h12, A0 rows), 83% of completed latencies are 0;
  - so the fitted median is 0.0, which is the default itself.

  The imputation was therefore a no-op exactly where it mattered: a failures-only pair still
  looked like the typical, fastest pair. Imputing the worst latency (TTL) would instead
  penalise in-flight pairs and confound with `never_routed`. The indicator keeps the
  information and lets a linear term separate "no completion yet" from "completes in the
  routing cycle".
- `env/agent_history.py` is unchanged: it still has 9 features. The indicator is built in
  `per_order_scorer.history_rows`.
- Test: `test_has_completed_indicator_separates_no_completion`. Routed pairs without a
  completion and 0-latency completed pairs are both ≥ 10 rows, and identical on raw latency
  (0.0). Their normalised `has_completed` differs (min vs max). Mutation M9 (indicator always
  0) fails it.

## Supervisor addition 2: the normaliser

- **Fit** (per column, over all fit rows): median = 50th percentile, scale = IQR (75th − 25th,
  numpy linear).
  - **Zero-spread rule:** IQR = 0 → the column's std; std = 0 → 1.0. The std step exists
    because a zero IQR is common. On the fixture (1,476 φ rows), 11 of 43 φ columns have
    IQR = 0: `active_upgrade`, `active_inventory_expansion`, `eligible`,
    `recent_completion_latency`, the completed / failed / rejected EMAs, `completion_latency`,
    `has_completed`, `assigned_units` and `assigned_orders`. A fixed 1.0 would leave
    own-turn units raw (tens of units).
  - Normalised values are clipped to ±5 (`CLIP`, hashed). On the fixture, `completion_rate`
    clips on 40% of rows: most rows sit at the prior 0.5, so its IQR is 0.03.
    `complete_qty_now` and `makeable_qty_now` clip on 17%. A full h100 fit should spread
    `completion_rate`; if it does not, it is effectively a 3-level feature.
  - **5.1 must record the clip and the rule in the pre-registration, and may revisit the
    clip.**
- All 43 φ and 13 ψ inputs are normalised, including own-turn and order features. D5's
  formula leaves those two unmarked; normalising them keeps σ = 0.05 in comparable units.
- The hash is SHA-256 over the canonical JSON of version, feature names, medians, scales,
  clip and n_rows. It changes with any value (tested). `from_dict` refuses another version or
  other feature names.
- **Rows 5.1 must fit it on:** the raw rows (`policy.sink = RawRows()`) of **A0** (no
  normaliser needed) played in the D6 **bar precompute** on the **training world-episodes
  only**, at horizon 100:
  - the 64 training worlds (23 atlas train + 41 fresh draws, 12 producers), after the
    infeasible-world replacement;
  - the training episodes and keys of the bar precompute;
  - never validation, held-out or monitor rows;
  - in agent-history mode, never shared-only.

  One φ row is recorded per (decision, eligible producer) and one ψ row per decision. The
  fit happens once, before any residual arm plays. Its hash goes into the pre-registration
  and every composite.
  - Expect early-episode empty histories to dominate (rate 0.5, `never_routed` 1), as the 4.2
    notes warned.
  - n_rows (decisions) on one h12 12-producer episode ≈ 370 decisions / 1,476 φ rows; a full
    h100 bar precompute gives ~10⁵–10⁶ φ rows. `fit` holds them in memory; at 43 float64
    each that is fine.

## Cost note for 5.4 (supervisor addition 7)

Medians of 3 episodes, local 2-core shared box (load 0.9–1.6), torch at 1 thread,
`entity_planner`, `shocks-i-smoke`. A2 uses θ ~ N(0, 0.05) and a fixture-fitted normaliser.
Times are the `run_episode` wall time only.

| world | H | TR | TS | A0 | A2 | A0 / TR | A2 / TR | A2 / TS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12-0060 | 100 | 3.22 s | 3.77 s | 3.06 s | 3.27 s | 0.95 | 1.02 | 0.87 |
| 12-0060 | 12 | 0.43 s | 0.50 s | 0.38 s | 0.46 s | 0.89 | 1.07 | 0.92 |
| 42-0031 (sparse_f14, train, shocked) | 12 | 5.98 s | 5.99 s | 4.08 s | 4.64 s | 0.68 | 0.78 | 0.77 |

- **Why below TS:** the policy skips the base forward on turns with no order (2,456 of 5,400
  turns hold orders on 12-0060 at h100), while TR and TS run it on every turn.
- **Before that skip:** A0 / TS = 0.97 and A2 / TR = 1.37 at h100. The residual itself costs
  ≈ 0.5 s per 12-producer h100 episode (φ / ψ assembly, history rows).
- **D10's assumption (≈ 1.3 × TR) holds with margin:** ≈ 1.0–1.1 × TR at 12 producers and
  0.8 × at 42. A shared-only ablation episode adds a mean over the product's agents per turn
  (not timed).
- 42-producer h100 was not timed locally (> 2 min). Episode dynamics differ between arms
  (TR completes 0 units on 42-0031 at h12), so per-episode ratios mix policy cost with state.

## Gotchas for 5.1 / 5.2

- **θ layout:** [w (43) | θ_τ | U (R × 43, row-major) | V (R × 13)]. A1's θ is A2's 44-prefix.
  - A2's ES start is `ARMS["A2"].initial_theta()`, with U from `default_rng(0).normal(0,
    0.1)` and V = 0. "θ = 0" in D5 means w = θ_τ = V = 0. Either start gives Δ ≡ 0.
  - Note: with U ≠ 0 and V = 0, ES perturbations on U alone do nothing to first order. The
    gradient flows through V first.
- **σ vector:** `arm.sigma()`, all 0.05 (w, θ_τ, U, V). ES perturbs θ + σ ⊙ ε.
- **Keys:** `set_draw_key(env, DrawKey(world, int(episode), int(replicate)))` per job, the
  same for every member of a generation (CRN). Build each member's policy fresh from θ:
  construction is cheap, and the base model is shared, read-only.
- **Non-finite guard:** a member θ that blows up raises `NonFiniteChoice` inside the
  episode. The ES driver should rank it worst (or treat it as a contract failure), never let
  it crash the pool.
- **Envs:** always `per_order_env` (decode + history), plus `per_order_pins` merged into
  `spec.json` pins. Never attach `RoutingTracer` to an env a fork or search will copy.
- **Feature-name order** is `PHI_FEATURES` / `PSI_FEATURES`. `kind` = 1 for a consumer order,
  0 for replenishment / investment. `quantity` and `tokens` are the `OrderInfo`'s (what
  decode routes). TTL remaining = max(0, `ORDER_TTL` − `order.cycle_counter`).
- **Shared-only ablation:** the mean is over every stream agent of the product, never-routed
  agents included (their rows are the prior). `never_routed` and `has_completed` then become
  fractions.
- A legacy ordering baseline or the TR scorer policy emits logits and is invalid in a
  `choice-v1` env. Bar arms (RR, MWW, TR, roster) keep running in `logits-v1` envs.
