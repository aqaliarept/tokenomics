## What the mode does
`order_es --mode noise-floor --bar-dir <bar> --probe-dir <EXP-0058> --probe-episodes
<EXP-0058>/artifacts/probe_episodes.csv --orderer-ckpt … --replicates 4 --workers N
--source-commit {SOURCE_COMMIT} --out-dir <dir>`

- **World-episodes:** the probe's two recorded minibatches, from EXP-0058's `probe.json`
  (`probe_world_episodes`), never re-derived. That is 12 world-episodes, each checked to be a
  training world-episode of the bar.
- **Jobs (`noise_jobs`):** A0 through the existing monitor-job path: `make_job(kind="noise",
  arm="A0", theta=[], replicate=r)`, played by `play` → `run_es_job`.
  - It runs at r = 0..K: 12 × 5 = 60 jobs at K = 4. Each replicate has its own draw key
    DrawKey(world, episode, r).
  - The rows (gate columns + trace + ES columns) go to `noise_floor.csv`. `play` checks every
    row's demand / shock digests against the bar's.
- **r0 reproduction (`reproduction_rows`):** the replayed A0(r0) must equal the bar's A0 row
  (units, death, holding). A mismatch is an `a0_reproduction` run failure: invalid, exit 2.
  It is compared only at the bar's horizon (a dry run at another horizon records nothing).
- **Check 2** (`check2.json`): `disqualifying(A0(r_k), bar row)` for k ≥ 1. This is D6's relative
  rule, unchanged, with the bar row being A0(r0). It gives the self-disqualification rate,
  overall and per flag, per world-episode, plus the flagged list.
- **Check 3** (`check3.csv`, `check3.json`): pair differences d_i = f(+i) − f(−i) against the noise
  of a difference between two independently keyed A0 episodes, √2 × A0's replicate SD (ddof 1,
  r0..rK), at two levels:
  - **minibatch:** d from EXP-0058's `probe_fitness.csv` (minibatch means), against the SD of A0's
    minibatch-mean fitness over replicates;
  - **world_episode:** d per world-episode from `probe_episodes.csv` (`we_fitness`), against A0's
    per-world-episode SD.

  Each row carries d, |d|, A0's SD, the noise, ratio = |d| / noise, and above 1× / 2×.
  `check3.json` holds the medians and shares per level × arm, plus A0's SDs. These are reported
  only; no threshold is registered.
  - **Caveat, recorded in the file:** members share one key (CRN), so re-keyed A0 noise is an
    *upper* reference for the draw noise inside a pair.
  - **The reading is one-sided** (registered before 6.1b's data, DIAGNOSTICS 25 check 3):
    - a ratio > 1 means the pair difference is above draw noise, which combined with sign
      disagreement across minibatches means heterogeneous;
    - a ratio ≤ 1 is **inconclusive**: it does not show "flat at σ".
  - **Deciding statistic (registered before 6.1b's data, DIAGNOSTICS 25):** the minibatch
    level, arm A1, the median ratio over its 24 pair differences (`check3.json`
    `summary["minibatch/A1"]["median_ratio"]`).
    - Median > 1 → "above noise", which with 6.1a's sign agreement at chance (5 / 12) reads
      heterogeneous.
    - Median ≤ 1 → inconclusive.
    - The world-episode level (144 ratios per arm), A2, and the shares above 1× / 2× are
      reported only.
  - A two-sided reading would need d's own spread across keys: the same pairs played at r1..rK,
    for example 4 A1 pairs × 12 world-episodes × K = 4 ≈ 384 jobs. That is the supervisor's
    call and is not implemented here.
- **Recompute (`--recompute`):** `noise_floor_readings` is a pure function of `noise_floor.csv`,
  the bar, the pinned world-episodes (from the out dir's `spec.json`) and the probe files.
  `--recompute` rebuilds `check2.json` / `check3.csv` / `check3.json` and byte-compares:
  MATCH → 0, MISMATCH → 2.
- **Real-run refusals (`noise_floor_problems`, exit 2 before any job):**
  - `order_es.real_run_problems`:
    - a 40-hex commit;
    - a non-dry bar with the registered bar pins;
    - the registered orderer `d25388bf…`;
    - the frozen prereg file's SHA;
    - the normaliser rule block and the `static_psi_clip_42` flag;
    - ≥ 50 training worlds;
  - the bar's atlas content hash = `220886a7…`;
  - the normaliser = EXP-0058's `2d574ec8a3df…`;
  - `probe_fitness.csv` = `379d5895f185…`, `probe.json` = `facfacfa8e75…`,
    `probe_episodes.csv` = `3c3e4df7895e…`;
  - `--replicates` = 4 (`NOISE_FLOOR_K`, the supervisor's K);
  - no filter (`--horizon`, `--max-world-episodes`).

  A resume onto other pins also exits 2. `--dry-run` allows `--max-world-episodes N --horizon
  12`.
