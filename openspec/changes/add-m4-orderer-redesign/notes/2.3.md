## Extrapolation (sizing.md §EXP-0057)
- Real job list: 639 jobs, in `longest_first` order (42 scorer → 42 rule → 12 scorer → 12 rule),
  greedily list-scheduled onto N workers. Pod s = 0.646 × local. Billed = vCPU × (10 min +
  wall). Wall includes 60 s of parent work and 3.5 s × 0.646 of worker start.
- Cell model (the estimate; each job at its size × arm cell on the loaded world): 11,376 local s
  (12: 2,336; 42: 9,040), so 2.04 pod compute vCPU-h. Longest job: 39 s on the pod.
- World model (check; the world's RR × the largest arm / RR ratio): 7,141 local s, 1.28 pod
  vCPU-h.

| shape | wall | billed (cell) | stop | billed (world) | billed at 3-min setup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cpu8 | 16.4 min | **3.52** | **7.0 (52 min)** | 2.75 | 2.58 |
| cpu16 | 8.7 min | 4.99 | 10.0 → cap 8 (30 min) | 4.23 | 3.12 |
| cpu32 | 4.9 min | 7.94 | 15.9 → cap 8 (15 min) | 7.17 | 4.21 |

- **Recommended: cpu8.** Estimate 3.5 billed vCPU-h, ≈ 16 min driver wall, hard stop 7.0 vCPU-h
  = 52 min of pod life, launch-fit 0 + 7.0 ≤ 95.
- **Reduction (MWW R = 1 if > 4): not triggered** (3.52 on cpu8). On cpu16 it would be triggered
  and would not help: 4.99 → 4.85, because setup is the excess there.
