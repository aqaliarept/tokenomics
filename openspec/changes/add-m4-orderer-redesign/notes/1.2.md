## Readings I chose (D3 / D7 ambiguities, stated in §d / §e too)
- "atlas `noise_floor` at the run's counts": the recorded per-world `noise_floor` as is (the run's
  counts are the confirm's). It includes σ_rep terms when the world's bar / reach argmax arm is
  stochastic, so for RR vs TR it can be larger than a pair-specific floor — hence the sensitivity line.
- "≥ 2 paired observations (episodes × replicates where the two arms differ)": read as the count of
  paired (episode, replicate) observations; RR and TR are R = 1, so = episodes. The other reading
  (count of non-zero differences) coincides here: 0 zero differences.
- No floor on rec_w (D3 defines none). Per-world means are equal-weight over episodes of replicate means
  (atlas D12 convention).
- §e SE = within-world sampling only (pooled SD of d_we around D_w, /√n per world, /√9 across worlds);
  between-world spread of D_w is excluded as a fixed property of the world set. A pooled MAD-sigma
  (28.3 → 0.77% at 4) is printed as a robust check only; with df 7 the two differ by 2.4×.
- §c: X / Y = the frozen exact values (0.75158…, 0.73017…), not the rounded task text (no row changes).
  Each MWW replicate row is paired with the other arm's single row of the same episode for discordance.
