# 4.3 draft: acceptance imitation (the turn heads fitted to today's defaults)

Draft for the supervisor, 2026-11-07. Once approved, it becomes pre-registration m6_prereg_v12, frozen before data.

**Question.** Can the GNN's turn heads (ADR-0062) reproduce today's acceptance, fund spend order and claim
scheduling, so the trained team runs no hand-written rule and loses nothing? This is the starting point the power
check (4.4) and training (4.5) need.

## Design
- **Start (4.0):**
  - **Producer:** trunk and investment heads frozen at EXP-0100's producer (EXP-0114 read `hold`, not `gain`).
  - **Orderer:** frozen at EXP-0100.
  - **Input buying:** as the start model's deterministic output.
- **Fitted:** only `TurnHeads`, from a fresh init:
  - fund: one score per request;
  - bid: an accept logit and a priority per routed bid;
  - claim: one priority per carried reservation or pending claim.
- **Labels:** deterministic, from the default turn policies (`default_turn_decision`, `default_fund_scores`):
  - **accept:** the auction's bit;
  - **order:** the default ranks:
    - carried reservations before pending claims before new bids, each price-descending;
    - funding requests in ADR-0025's survival-first order.

  Only the order matters, so priorities and fund scores are fitted with a pairwise logistic ranking loss within
  each turn. Accept bits use BCE.
- **Data:**
  - **Train:** the start stack plays M4's 112 training world-episodes × replicates 0 / 1 with the default turns,
    horizon 100, 12 producers. Each fund turn and accept turn records its features (the trunk inputs plus
    `fund_features` / `accept_features`) and its label.
  - **Held out:** the 16 monitor world-episodes × 2 replicates.
  - **Trunk caching:** the trunk is frozen, so its embeddings are computed once per recorded turn. The fit only
    runs the heads.
- **Offline gate** (held-out turns; pre-registered):
  - accept agreement ≥ 99% of bids;
  - pairwise rank agreement ≥ 99%, separately for claims and for fund requests;
  - reported: the share of turns whose full schedule (accept set and order) equals the default's.
  - If it fails: no closed loop. Diagnose (per-feature errors, DIAGNOSTICS 34 style).
- **Closed loop** (only if the offline gate passes):
  - The candidate: the start stack with greedy GNN turn heads. The reference: the start stack with default turns.
  - Played on the 16 monitor world-episodes × r0 / r1, paired keys, M5's floors, as the v10 / v11 gate.
  - **`pass`:** g ≥ −F, no more deaths, and run-down and starvation shares no worse.
  - **`fail`:** otherwise.
  - Reported: 42 producers on the same rule.

## Sizing (timed 2026-11-07, local, horizon 20, EXP-0100 models, single thread)
**Cost per episode-cycle against today's stack without turns:**
- default turns: 1.04–1.08×;
- GNN turn heads (3 trunk passes per producer-cycle: invest, fund, accept): 1.26–1.60×.

**Collection.** EXP-0113's rollouts ran ≈ 5.6 s per h100 episode per core (384 episodes in ≈ 135 s on 16
workers). With default turns plus feature recording (≈ 1.5×): ≈ 8.5 s.
- Training: 224 episodes ≈ 0.5 vCPU-h.
- Held-out: 32 episodes ≈ 0.1 vCPU-h.

**Fit.** The trunk is frozen and cached, so only the heads run.
- About 224 × 100 × 12 ≈ 270k accept turns plus 270k fund turns, with ≈ 3–10 bids or claims each.
- At ≈ 1 ms per turn for the head and loss, one epoch ≈ 9 vCPU-min. 20 epochs ≈ 3 vCPU-h, plus the trunk cache
  (one pass per turn, ≈ 0.5 vCPU-h).

**Closed loop.**
- 12 producers: 2 arms × 32 episodes at ≈ 1.5× (GNN heads) ≈ 0.2 vCPU-h.
- 42 reported, on the 42-producer world-episodes the ladder already reads (count to be fixed in v12): ≈ 0.4 vCPU-h
  at ≈ 3× the 12-producer cost per episode.

**Total ≈ 5 vCPU-h; stop 12** (tasks.md: ≈ 8, stop 16).

**Effect on the rest of the ladder:**
- Learned turns make every RL rollout 1.26–1.60× slower. So 4.4 grows from ≈ 6 to ≈ 8 vCPU-h, and 4.5's
  training from ≈ 35 to ≈ 50 vCPU-h.
- M6-A then totals ≈ 3.4 (EXP-0115) + 5 + 8 + 50 + 8 (validation and held-out) ≈ 75 vCPU-h, against 70.
- **Caveat from EXP-0114:** its iterations took ≈ 5.7 min against EXP-0113's ≈ 4, with the same settings
  (cause not measured: host or the policy's growth). At that pace 4.5 is nearer ≈ 70 than 50, and M6-A nearer
  ≈ 95. 4.3's own cost moves little (≈ 6 instead of 5). 4.5 must be re-sized from 4.4's measured iteration
  time before it is registered.
- **Two ways to fit, decided before 4.5:**
  - reuse the fund turn's trunk pass for the accept turn (no producer state changes between them; the orders do
    change, so this needs a check that the view is equal or a recorded approximation): ≈ 1.15× instead of 1.5×;
  - or ask the user for ≈ 10 more vCPU-h.
