## A. What trains and how each head explores
User decision 2: every producer head trains on-policy, played in training as it is deployed.
- **The rule:** one model, and the same heads and code path in training and deployment. Deployment plays the greedy
  version of each head's distribution; training samples from it.
- **What drifted before:** DIAGNOSTICS 37 was a *different* head being played (the frozen start's replenish), and
  that cannot happen here.

| head | deployed (greedy) | training sample | temperature / noise, set offline before data |
|---|---|---|---|
| investment gates (3) | logit > 0 | Bernoulli(σ(logit / T_g)) | T_g = v9 TEMPS (5% flips per gate) |
| replenish (per input row) | frac = σ(z) | frac = σ(z + σ_r ε), ε ~ N(0, 1) | σ_r: mean \|Δ frac\| = 0.02 per row |
| fund order | requests by score | Plackett–Luce on score / τ_f (Gumbel-top-k) | τ_f: 5% of adjacent learnable pairs swap |
| accept-turn order | items by priority | Plackett–Luce on priority / τ_p | τ_p: 5% of adjacent learnable pairs swap |
| accept bits | a > 0 (pass in the greedy order) | Bernoulli(σ(a / T_a)), a from the pass run **in the sampled order** | T_a: 5% flips |

**Calibration.** All temperatures are set offline on the start policy's recorded decisions: EXP-0126's held-out
records for the turns, EXP-0105's monitor samples for gates and replenish.

**Log-probability of one producer decision**, the sum over the heads that acted:
- gates: Bernoulli;
- replenish: Gaussian in logit space;
- fund: the Plackett–Luce log-likelihood of the sampled order;
- accept: the Plackett–Luce log-likelihood of the sampled order + the Bernoulli bits given that order.

**Why the replenish noise is small.** EXP-0111: with Gaussian replenish noise, 99.4–99.7% of the producer's score was
replenish noise and the return signal vanished. Here σ_r is set so that replenish moves about as much per decision as
a 5% gate flip does. **4.4 reports every head family's share of the score**, so this is checked, not assumed.

## B. Update (4.5)
- **Algorithm:** E3 PPO-clip with a KL anchor to the start (F40 + v15), as v10 / v11: 96 seeds × 4 siblings per
  iteration, team return-to-go with the leave-one-out sibling baseline per cycle, and the cycle's advantage on every
  decision of that cycle.
- **What it covers:** all producer parameters (trunk, gates, replenish, turn heads) in one model.
- **The KL term per head:**
  - Bernoulli: exact;
  - Gaussian: exact;
  - Plackett–Luce: the sampled log-ratio estimate.
- **Unchanged from v11:** clip 0.2, lr 3e-4, grad clip 1.0, 100 steps of 256 decisions per iteration.
  - KL coefficient: 0.04, unless 4.4's gradient sizes call for another value (pre-registered in v17).

## C. Reward: re-weighted toward consumer units (user decision 3)
Φ = s·units − λ·run-down − μ·starving − ν·death, with EXP-0110's calibration (λ 0.736, μ 0.095, ν = H).

**Proposal: λ₄.₅ = λ / 4 = 0.184; μ and ν unchanged.**
- EXP-0116: 72% of the Φ gain was run-down relief and 19% units (+27.3 vs +7.3).
- Quartering λ makes the run-down contribution ≈ the units contribution, so units lead. Run-down stays as a guard.
- The gates' run-down clause (no worse than the start) still guards it at evaluation.

## D. Gates and reads (4.5), as v11
- **Gate:** the greedy candidate vs the start (F40 + v15, greedy) with M5's floors.
  - At iterations 15 / 20 / 30 stop-only; at **40 deciding**.
  - `gain`: g > F and side effects no worse; `hold`: |g| ≤ F; `worse`: otherwise.
- **Gate set:** the monitor episodes are used up (EXP-0113–0126), so the gate plays the 16 monitor worlds × 2 fresh
  episodes (a new stage) × r0 / r1. The same set at every look, so the looks are not independent and only 40 is
  read.
- **Then, as tasks.md:**
  - validation: L5 worlds, 12 deciding, 42 reported;
  - the held-out read: a second comparison on that set, noted.

## E. 4.4: the power check (EXP-0112 style, before 4.5)
- **Start and exploration:** F40 + v15, with section A's exploration.
- **Per seed:** G = 4 siblings, rollouts with per-decision projected scores over all producer parameters
  (`m6_stage0` projections), estimators E1–E4.
- **True gradient size:** ±ε along K random directions at a per-direction mean KL of 0.01 under section A's policy.
- **Reading (v9's rule):** `signal` if at 12 some estimator's producer cosine beats its permutation null's q95 and its
  n80 at the measured |∇J| is ≤ 96 seeds per half; else `absent`.
- **Reported:**
  - each head family's share of the squared score (gates / replenish / fund order / accept order / accept bits);
  - flip and swap rates;
  - deaths.
- **`signal` licenses 4.5.**
