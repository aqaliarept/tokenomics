# Task 5.2: screen and pilot timing probe (design D3)

Not committed. Implementer: impl-gate-v3-2-1. No pod was started.

## What was delivered

- **`/srv/research/tokenomics/openspec/changes/add-gate-v3/probes/timing_screen_v3.py`**, the
  timing probe.
  - It times `gate_cli.run_job` on jobs that `gate_cli.build_jobs` builds from the Drafts
    registry v3: one roster-member job per call, `--workers 1`, horizon 100 by default.
  - It appends one JSONL line per job: the job, its seconds, units, death, load average and
    MemAvailable before and after, and peak RSS.
  - `--guard-load` / `--guard-mem-mb` refuse to start a job on a busy box. I used
    `--guard-load 4` after the first stall.
- **`/srv/research/tokenomics/openspec/changes/add-gate-v3/probes/timing_screen_v3.jsonl`**, the
  raw timings: 54 lines.
  - 52 are at horizon 100: 7 worlds × 7 members (2 added in review round 1), plus 3 determinism
    reruns tagged `rerun`.
  - 2 are horizon-12 scout lines tagged `h12-scout`, which are not used.
- **`/srv/research/tokenomics/openspec/changes/add-gate-v3/probes/extrapolate_screen_v3.py`**,
  the extrapolation.
  - It builds the real job lists with `gate_cli.build_jobs` for the pilot, the screen without
    reductions, with reduction 1, and with reductions 1 + 2.
  - It prices every job by the class and upper models and simulates the pool in submission
    order.
  - Its outputs are `extrapolate_screen_v3.json` (estimate) and
    `extrapolate_screen_v3.central.json` (`--central`).
- **`/srv/research/tokenomics/openspec/changes/add-gate-v3/sizing.md`**: §pilot and §screen. This
  file is new; the change had no sizing.md before. It follows the format of
  add-m4-orderer-redesign's sizing.md.

  - On cpu32 it should take about 15 min of driver wall and bill about 13.5 vCPU-h on the 10-min
    setup basis (8.7 on the observed 1-min setup). The hard stop is 27.0.
  - On cpu16 it is about 29 min, 10.5 billed and a stop of 20.9.
  - That is more than task 6.1 registered (cpu16, ≈ 7 billed, stop 14). The 42-producer f14

    routers such as `proportional` take about twice as long as `round_robin` there.
  - Both shapes stay under 40, so no reduction is needed.
- **Screen.**
  - Without reductions the stop would be 52.5 on cpu32 and 46.4 on cpu16, both over 40.
  - D3's reduction 1 (shocked region worlds at 5 episodes) brings cpu32 only to 46.2.
  - Reductions 1 + 2 (also reuse EXP-0047's v2 rows after the exact-reproduction check) bring
    cpu32 to 18.8 billed, about 25 min of driver wall, and a stop of 37.6.
  - Recommended: **cpu32 with reductions 1 + 2.**
  - On the observed 1-min setup basis, reduction 1 alone would fit cpu32 (stop 36.6).
- **Determinism.** The shocked `ws_wide` world gives identical units on a rerun for
  `round_robin` and for both stochastic members (11743, 12926, 1830).
- **Reproduction.** On the v2 world, the four probed members EXP-0047 also ran give its units
  exactly.
- **Tranche G1 (65).** The pilot and screen launch-fit checks pass. The teacher read's check
  would miss: 13.5 + 18.8 + 40 = 72.3. This is for the supervisor.

## Departures from the task text (each for the reviewer)

1. **An extra member, `stock_aware` × `proportional`.** The task lists six members. The 88-member
   roster has 22 `stock_aware` / `obs_stock_aware` members, whose LEGACY-contract cost was
   otherwise unmeasured. One extra job per world prices them directly. The probe's default
   member list is still the task's six.
2. **The most loaded worlds, more than one per `ws_loaded` cell.** The task says "one calm and
   one shocked development world" per family (one dense, one f4 for `ws_loaded`).
   - For `ws_wide` I took the gate world with the largest k_c × m_q in each cell.
   - For `ws_loaded`, round 0 took one world per cell inside the dense / f4 split and wrongly
     called it the cell's maximum (review round 1). Each `ws_loaded` cell now has its most
     loaded dense and its most loaded f4 world timed: four worlds instead of the task's two.
   - Each member is priced at the max over its cell's timed worlds, a load-based upper estimate
     for the cell.
3. **The 0.646 check is a speed comparison, not a recomputation.** There is no pod in this task,
   so the "0.646 check" compares today's local speed on the world the factor was derived on with
   the original measurement. The ratio is 1.197, and the estimate applies it in the worse
   direction. The central variant reads it as box slowdown, which is about 15–17% lower. The v2
   units also reproduce EXP-0047's rows.
4. **The W2 roster and the reserves are modelled.** Wave 2's real membership depends on wave 1's
   winners. I modelled D3's "≈ 13 members, 4 stochastic" as `entity_planner` × 11 plus two
   `stock_aware` members, one of them stochastic. Reserve sub-waves are not in the totals; a
   one-reserve-per-region-group contingency is given in Sizing and stays within the stop.
5. **Two setup bases.** As asked, billed is given on both the observed setup (about 1 min) and the
   10-min planning basis. The stop is 2 × the planning basis. The recommendation follows the
   planning basis, which is the convention so far, and Sizing states the observed-basis
   alternative.
6. **Task 6.1's resources line and D10 are not edited.** The pilot re-size (stop 14 → 20.9 on
   cpu16 or 27.0 on cpu32), the screen's reductions and the G1 miss are written in Sizing for the
   supervisor.

       recommended before either).
