## Gotchas for 9.1

- **Pod shape (supervisor re-decision).**
  - **Primary:** 4 vCPU secure, `--workers 4`, stop 7.0 billed vCPU-h = 105 min of pod life.
  - **Fallback:** if no 4 vCPU secure capacity is available, 8 vCPU secure, `--workers 8`, with
    its own stop of 10 billed vCPU-h = 75 min of pod life (covers the × 2 longest job,
    70.7 min). The first decision's shared 6.5 stop (48.75 min on 8 vCPU) is gone.
  - **Never community.**
  - **At the wall:** stop the pod, pull the partial rows, and do not resume without a new
    supervisor decision. A running A job is lost, because a job is indivisible.
  `cpu16` would bill about 10 vCPU-h central and 19 at × 2, because at most 6 jobs search and
  after ~19 min only 2 run.
- **Wall is set by A, not T.** 0344's A is ~35 pod min. A job cannot be split, so a stop
  expressed as wall must be ≥ that × 2 (~71 min) plus overhead.
- **Driver order matters on 4 vCPU.** The order is S, A, B, T per world. 0344's A only starts at
  ~8.5 min, after 0111's T frees a worker, so the wall is 44 min, not 35. Longest-first would
  reach 35 min; that is a driver change, not made.
- **T is rerun.** Pass `--reuse-t` with EXP-0053 staged (its `spec.json` + `artifacts/episodes.csv`)
  so `t_reproduction` compares units. Stage under the git-ignored `tokenomics/_pod_ckpts/`, not
  `_pod_inputs/`: an untracked file there fails `--verify-source-commit` (EXP-0053 RESULT
  "Staging path changed").
- **Pod speed.** The box runs at the 0.646 calibration speed today (`rr` 0.83 s). The 6.3
  cross-check (T 0060 cycle 30: 24.3 s against 23.6) reproduced.
- **Probe caveat.** The probe cells play `round_robin` before the searched cycle, so the state at
  cycle 30/60/90 is the floor's trajectory, not A's own.
- **Per-cycle cost is NOT flat (review concern 1).** In EXP-0053, T's pod search seconds per
  10-cycle decile were:
  - 0060: 129 / 122 / 116, then 77–87.
  - 0344: 139 / 144 / 98, then 95–118.
  - 0111: 50–61 over cycles 0–59, then 35–37.

  The probe's cycles, against the episode mean on T, give cycle-pick factors 0.84 (0060),
  0.88 (0344) and 1.33 (0111). Corrected that way:
  - A is 36.9 / 9.8 / 40.2 pod min.
  - 4 vCPU at × 2 needs 99.3 min of pod life (97.3 min of driver wall + 2 min overhead;
    6.62 billed). That is just above the 97.5 min wall and the 6.5 vCPU-h stop, which on 4 vCPU

    The re-decided stop, 7.0 vCPU-h = 105 min, covers it.
