## Arm A per-stream cost (local, measured once)

- Dry run: world 12-0060, horizon 12, `shocks-i-smoke`, `--max-search-actions 3`, `--workers 1`.
  It logged 648 teacher decisions. Only the first 3 were searched because of the cap.
- Seconds per searched stream: 0.58, 0.45 and 0.49 (about 0.5 s). Each searched stream had
  4 candidates and 4 scenarios at the first confidence checkpoint. Episode `search_seconds` was
  2.94. The run's other numbers: 393 `searched_streams` in the telemetry, A units 124, S 81,
  B 90.
- Without the cap, A at horizon 12 did not finish within the 2-minute command limit (killed
  after about 105 s; S had finished, A had not). The likely cause: the portable producer is a
  batch-1 torch policy, and it also plays every rollout (tested by
  `test_arm_a_plays_the_portable_producer`). A therefore costs more per rollout than PoC-A's
  `d2`, which uses `entity_planner`. 8.2 must measure this with the 6.3 composed-cost method
  (streams per cycle × candidates × seconds per searched stream, plus sampler time at a
  horizon-100 history, × 0.646 local-to-pod). Do not assume EXP-0053's `d2` timings hold for A.
- Contested decisions carry a candidate count of 4, 8 or 12 (4 is the most common; 12 is rare).
